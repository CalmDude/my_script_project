## The Larsson Line Indicator

### What is the Larsson Line?

The Larsson Line uses **4 Smoothed Moving Averages (SMMA)** to identify three distinct trend states:

**Four Lines:**
- **V1 (Fast):** 15-period SMMA
- **M1:** 19-period SMMA
- **M2:** 25-period SMMA
- **V2 (Slow):** 29-period SMMA

### Three Trend States

#### 1. BULLISH (Gold/Yellow Cloud)
- All moving averages aligned
- Clear uptrend confirmed
- **Action: BUY signals are valid**

#### 2. NEUTRAL (Gray Cloud)
- Moving averages crossing/mixed
- Transition period, uncertainty
- **Action: WAIT - Do not enter new positions**

#### 3. BEARISH (Blue Cloud)
- Downtrend confirmed
- V1 < V2 with alignment
- **Action: EXIT positions or stay in cash**

### Key Insight
The Larsson Line turns GRAY during normal pullbacks - this is EXPECTED behavior. For long-term investing, we use **WEEKLY Larsson** to filter out this noise.

## Fibonacci Retracement Levels

### The Three Key Levels

After a price move from swing low to swing high, stocks typically retrace to:

1. **38.2% Retracement** - Shallow pullback (strong trends)
2. **50.0% Retracement** - Mid-level support (moderate pullbacks)
3. **61.8% Retracement** - Golden ratio, strongest support (deep pullbacks)

### How to Draw Fibonacci

**On Daily Chart:**
1. Identify clear swing LOW (start of move)
2. Identify clear swing HIGH (peak of move)
3. Draw Fibonacci from LOW → HIGH
4. Tool will display retracement levels automatically

### Dynamic Retracement

As the trend progresses:
- When price makes new higher high, **redraw Fibonacci** from that new high
- This keeps your entry zones aligned with current price action
- Always use most recent major swing points

### Gray Zone Warning

**DO NOT BUY** between Fibonacci levels (e.g., 42%, 55%, 64%)
- No clear invalidation point
- Lower probability setups
- Wait for exact levels

## Position Sizing - Golden Ratio Allocation

### The Golden Ratio Weighted Approach

**Philosophy:** Allocate most capital at strongest support (61.8% level)

**For $100,000 total allocation to a stock:**

#### Level 1: 61.8% Fibonacci (BEST VALUE)
- **Allocation: 50% of capital** = $50,000
- Deepest retracement, strongest support
- Golden ratio level - highest probability
- If this breaks, trend may be invalidating anyway

#### Level 2: 50.0% Fibonacci (GOOD VALUE)
- **Allocation: 30% of capital** = $30,000
- Mid-level support
- Balanced entry point
- Still attractive risk/reward

#### Level 3: 38.2% Fibonacci (EARLY ENTRY)
- **Allocation: 20% of capital** = $20,000
- Shallowest retracement
- Highest risk of continued decline
- Smallest position appropriate

### Why This Allocation?

1. **Better average entry** if all levels hit
2. **61.8% is statistically strongest** Fibonacci level
3. **Limits exposure** at weaker shallow retracements
4. **Maximum capital** deployed at best value
5. **Inverse pyramid** matches probability to capital

### Alternative: Equal Weight (Simpler)

If you prefer simplicity:
- 38.2%: 33.3%
- 50.0%: 33.3%
- 61.8%: 33.4%

### Handling Retests

**If price returns to same level again:**

#### Bearish Retest (Failed Level)
- Price rallied weakly, now back at same level with selling
- **Action:** Hold existing position, DO NOT add
- Wait for deeper levels

#### Bullish Retest (Successful Level)
- Price rallied strongly, pulling back on low volume
- **Action:** Can add 10-15% more (not full allocation)
- Example: Had 20% at 38.2%, add 15% more = 35% total at this level

**Key Rule:** Never exceed 40% total allocation at any single Fibonacci level

## Volume Confirmation

### Why Volume Matters

**Volume reveals true market sentiment:**
- High volume down = Real selling pressure
- Declining volume down = Profit-taking, not panic
- High volume up from support = Strong buying conviction

### Daily Volume Analysis for Pullbacks

**Look at the DAILY volume bars during pullback days:**

#### Healthy Pullback Pattern (BUY Signal)
```
Day 1 of pullback: High volume (profit-taking starts)
Day 2: Lower volume
Day 3: Even lower volume (selling exhausting)
Day 4-5: Very low volume (bulls waiting)
Bounce day: HIGH VOLUME (buyers defending level)
```

**Visual:** Volume bars getting progressively **shorter** day by day

#### Unhealthy Pullback Pattern (AVOID)
```
Day 1: High volume down
Day 2: HIGHER volume down (escalating)
Day 3: Still elevated volume
Pattern: Institutional selling, distribution
```

**Visual:** Volume bars staying **tall** or getting **taller**

### Practical Check

**Before buying at Fibonacci level:**

1. Look at last 3-5 daily volume bars
2. Are they declining? ✓ = Healthy
3. Are they increasing? ✗ = Warning
4. Is bounce day volume high? ✓ = Confirmation

### Weekly Volume

**Use weekly volume for:**
- Long-term trend strength validation
- Rising price on rising weekly volume = Strong trend
- **NOT useful** for analyzing 3-5 day pullbacks

## Risk Management

### Stop Loss Placement

#### Initial Stop Loss (Fibonacci Entries)

**For 61.8% Entry:**
- Stop: Below daily 200 SMA OR 10% below entry
- Whichever is closer to entry price

**For 50% Entry:**
- Stop: Below 61.8% level OR daily 200 SMA

**For 38.2% Entry:**
- Stop: Below 50% level

#### Layered Stop Strategy (Recommended)

**Position at 61.8% Fibonacci ($387 example):**

1. **Soft Stop:** Daily 200 SMA ($369)
   - Exit 50% of position
   - Warning level, not catastrophic

2. **Hard Stop:** 10-15% above Weekly 200 SMA
   - Exit remaining 50%
   - Approaching ultimate support

3. **Nuclear Stop:** Weekly 200 SMA break
   - Exit 100% if somehow still holding
   - Never let this happen in practice

### Position Sizing Per Stock

**Maximum allocation to single stock:**
- Conservative: 5-10% of total portfolio
- Moderate: 10-15% of total portfolio
- Aggressive: 15-20% of total portfolio

**For $1M portfolio:**
- 10% allocation = $100,000 to one stock
- Split across Fibonacci levels: $20K, $30K, $50K

### Portfolio-Level Risk

**Diversification:**
- 8-12 positions ideal for long-term portfolio
- All positions must have weekly Larsson GOLD
- Different sectors/industries
- Stagger entry timing

**Maximum Drawdown:**
- Individual position: 15% max loss
- Portfolio: 20% max drawdown before reassessment

### The 200 SMA Shield

**Think of 200 SMAs as safety zones:**

```
Weekly 200 SMA ($269) - NUCLEAR LEVEL
    ↑ 10-15% buffer zone
Hard Stop Zone (~$300) - EXIT REMAINING
    ↑ 
Daily 200 SMA ($369) - WARNING LEVEL
    ↑ Reduce 50%
Entry Zones (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) - BUY LEVELS
    ↑ Normal trading range
```

**As long-term investor:** You should exit well before reaching weekly 200 SMA if following the system properly.
